Building software solo leaves many blind spots. You become so used to an app's idiosyncrasies that it's hard to put yourself in the shoes of new users. Imagine my surprise when [Jim Pankey](https://jimpankey.com) answered my cold intro online to talk about [bluegrass book](https://bluegrassbook.com/T). Talk about the ultimate beta test!

Jim is a giant in the bluegrass banjo community. He's released hundreds of videos teaching people not just how to play banjo songs, but how to think about playing banjo so that they can continue to teach themselves. His videos were my very first introduction to banjo. Jim was generous with his time, and I came away from our chat more motivated than ever to continue working on this project.

I reached out to Jim because he's had a long second career in bluegrass teaching lessons, then eventually expanding to online content creation. He understands the space and how people can make a living online with bluegrass. I'm currently navigating a career transition and thinking about whether or not I can build a business.

After our conversation, and his encouragement, we decided the best path forward was a more public "reveal" about Bluegrass Book and its capabilities. Jim even offered to collaborate on some content to spread the word. I am beyond grateful!

I think the first step here is to talk about where we're at with Bluegrass Book, and what's coming down the line and what currently exists. There are three ways to stay up to date with what's going on:

- [Take the tour](https://bluegrassbook.com/T): a round trip interactive tour of Bluegrass Book features!
- A new "[what's new](https://bluegrassbook.com/whats-new/)" page: log to show what has been shipped recently, and what is coming soon!
- [A guide](https://bluegrassbook.com/guide/) to all the features of Bluegrass Book!

In our short call, Jim already had tons of great ideas, which I've gotta write down for posterity, or I am gonna forget!

- Be noisier about confirming actions - he submitted a tab, but then didn't get enough visual confirmation that it was now on the index.
- People will pay for the right features, don't be afraid to ask.
- Let people know how long it will take for stuff to appear on the index so they know when to look out for it.
- Track corrections in addition to song submissions for scores
- Add capo'd chords
- Add an about + incoming changes page

It was so energizing to discuss this work with Jim, and I am looking forward to future collaboration. Stay tuned!
